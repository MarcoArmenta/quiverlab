**Goal.** A field-general, engine-independent Lie-theoretic classification of the first

## Mathematical foundation (the definitions, exactly — the plan's ground truth)

Throughout `A` is a finite-dimensional associative unital `k`-algebra with structure-constant
tensor `T` (`e_i · e_j = Σ_t T[i][j][t] · e_t`, the quiverlab internal currency `A.T`), `d =
A.dim`, `k = A.domain` an exact `Domain`. Composition is left-to-right; every `k` is exact.

### 1. HH¹ as the outer-derivation Lie algebra

- **Derivation.** A `k`-linear `D: A → A` with `D(ab) = D(a)b + aD(b)`. As a `d×d` matrix over
  `k` (column `j` = coordinates of `D(e_j)`), the Leibniz law is the linear system, for all
  `i,j,k`:
  ```
  Σ_m T[i][j][m]·D[k][m]  −  Σ_p D[p][i]·T[p][j][k]  −  Σ_q D[q][j]·T[i][q][k]  =  0.
  ```
  `Der(A) = nullspace` of this `d³ × d²` matrix (the unit forces `D(1)=0` automatically).
- **Inner derivation.** `ad_x(a) = xa − ax`. `ad_{e_i}` has column `j = (T[i][j][k] −
  T[j][i][k])_k`. `Inn(A) = span{ad_{e_i}}` is an **ideal** of `Der(A)`, since
  `[D, ad_x] = ad_{D(x)}` for any derivation `D`.
- **HH¹ = Der(A)/Inn(A).** The standard identification of the first Hochschild cohomology with
  outer derivations (Cartan–Eilenberg; Gerstenhaber). **Verified anchor (Live-verified below):
  `dim(Der/Inn)` equals `dim HH¹` from `A.hochschild_cohomology` on every test example** — the
  cross-engine oracle that the vector space is correct.
- **The bracket.** The Gerstenhaber bracket restricted to `HH¹ ⊗ HH¹ → HH¹` is exactly the
  commutator `[D,E] = D∘E − E∘D` of representative derivations (Gerstenhaber 1963, degree 1),
  which descends to `Der/Inn` because `Inn` is an ideal. **This is the primary route** — no
  resolution, no window: pure matrix commutator on the `Der/Inn` representatives, over any
  exact field. `HH1Lie` records its bracket structure constants **in the `Der/Inn`
  representative basis** (basis-provenance tag `"der_inn"`).

### 2. Field-general invariants (any characteristic; basis-independent)

Let `L = HH¹`, presented as `Der` modulo the ideal `Inn`. Compute entirely with subspaces of
`Der` that contain `Inn` (so no explicit complement basis is needed for the verdicts):

- **Derived series.** `L⁽⁰⁾ = L`; `L⁽ᵏ⁺¹⁾ = [L⁽ᵏ⁾, L⁽ᵏ⁾]`. In `Der`-terms:
  `V₀ = Der`, `V_{k+1} = ⟨[V_k, V_k]⟩ + Inn`; `dim L⁽ᵏ⁾ = rank(V_k) − dim Inn`. **`L` solvable
  ⟺ the series reaches `Inn`** (i.e. `dim L⁽ᵏ⁾ = 0`).
- **Lower central series.** `L^{[0]} = L`; `L^{[k+1]} = [L, L^{[k]}]`; `W_{k+1} = ⟨[Der, W_k]⟩
  + Inn`. **`L` nilpotent ⟺ the series reaches `Inn`.**
- **Abelian** ⟺ `[L,L] = 0` (⟺ `⟨[Der,Der]⟩ ⊆ Inn`); **perfect** ⟺ `[L,L] = L`.
- These are **basis-independent** and computable in **any characteristic** (no Killing form).
  Pinned unconditionally.

### 3. Characteristic-0 classification (HARD gate: `k.characteristic == 0`)

The following rest on the **Killing form** `κ(x,y) = tr(ad x ∘ ad y)` and Cartan's / Levi's /
Weyl's theorems — the whole classification machinery (Cartan's solvability criterion, the
existence of a Levi complement, Weyl's complete reducibility). **These are theorems of
characteristic 0; there is NO positive-characteristic analogue** — so the classification
algorithm is unjustified for *any* `char > 0`, regardless of `dim`. The gate is therefore
`A.domain.characteristic == 0`, checked before any of these run; otherwise a loud
`QuiverlabError`. (This is **not** the associative Dickson / Cohen–Ivanyos–Wales `char > dim`
allowance of `modules/decompose.py`: that trace-form bound is a genuine sufficient condition in
positive characteristic, whereas here **no** `char > 0` bound rescues Cartan/Levi/Weyl — the
gate is `char == 0` outright. §4 records the mathematically rich char-p phenomenon this walls
off: `HH¹(k[x]/(x^p)) = W₁`, on which `κ` is degenerate and Levi's theorem has no content.)

- **Solvable radical.** `rad(L) = [L,L]^⊥` = `{x ∈ L : κ(x, y) = 0 ∀ y ∈ [L,L]}` (de Graaf,
  char 0). The largest solvable ideal. **Live-verified**: `rad(sl₂) = 0`,
  `rad(sl₂ ⋉ k) = k` (dim 1), `rad(k[x]/(x^n)) = L` (the whole solvable algebra).
- **Levi decomposition.** `L = rad(L) ⋉ S`, `S ≅ L/rad(L)` semisimple (Levi's theorem, char 0).
  `dim S = dim L − dim rad`. Reported as the Levi factor's **isomorphism type** (via its own
  Killing form / simple-ideal decomposition), NOT necessarily lifted into `L` (the abstract
  type of `L/rad` carries every invariant the card asks for).
- **sl₂-count `m`.** The number of simple ideals of `S` isomorphic to a **form of `sl₂`**
  (3-dimensional simple). On the Eisele–Raedschelders class (tame/finite type) `S` is a sum of
  `sl₂`'s, so `m = dim S / 3` there; in general `m` counts 3-dimensional simple ideals.
  **The pinned cases (`kK₂`, `T(kK₂)`, the solvable `k[x]/(x^n)`) are all `dim S ∈ {0, 3}` —
  they do NOT exercise the general many-factor simple-ideal decomposition**; the worker must not
  silently claim generality (see Task 3's honest-scope fallback and Open risk 1).
  **Base-change provenance (binding), gated on the EXACT ARITHMETIC field, not the formal
  `is_algebraically_closed` flag:** over a field whose exact arithmetic is not genuine
  algebraically-closed-field arithmetic a 3-dimensional simple ideal may be an **anisotropic
  form** of `sl₂` (split only over the closure); `m` counts split-`sl₂` factors over the field
  as computed and the report states that base change to `k̄` may increase `m`. **Every quiverlab
  char-0 domain has QQ arithmetic** — `QQ` itself and, crucially, the DEFAULT field
  **`CC` ("computing exactly in QQ")**, whose `is_algebraically_closed` is `True` *formally* but
  whose arithmetic is exact **QQ**: an anisotropic-over-QQ factor could be undercounted with no
  caveat if the note were gated on the formal flag (the very bug this ruling closes). So the
  `base_change_note` is attached for **CC, QQ, `GF(p)` and `GF(pⁿ)` alike** — worded honestly,
  the CC case stating "formally closed, exact arithmetic in QQ" and never a fabricated
  "algebraically closed" verdict on the count (the P62 wording precedent).
- **Toral rank.** The **rank of the semisimple Levi factor** `S` — `dim` of a Cartan
  subalgebra of `S` (`= m` when `S = sl₂^m`, since `rank(A₁) = 1`). Honest-scope: the broader
  "maximal torus of `L` including ad-semisimple elements of `rad(L)`" is **out of scope** (the
  grading element of `k[x]/(x^n)` is such a toral element in the radical); the reported toral
  rank is the semisimple-Levi rank, with that boundary stated.

### 4. The char-p boundary is real: `k[x]/(x^n)` and the Jacobson–Witt algebra

`A = k[x]/(x^n)` is commutative, so `Inn(A) = 0` and `HH¹(A) = Der(A)`. A derivation is
determined by `D(x) = Σ_{i≥0} a_i x^i`, with the single constraint from `x^n = 0`:
`D(x^n) = n·a_0·x^{n-1} = 0`, i.e. `n·a_0 = 0` in `k`.
- **`char k ∤ n`** (includes all of char 0): `a_0 = 0` forced, `dim HH¹ = n − 1`, basis
  `D_i : x ↦ x^{i+1}` (`i = 0…n−2`), bracket `[D_i, D_j] = (j−i)·D_{i+j}` — the **positive**
  part of the Witt algebra with the grading element `D_0` (Euler operator). **SOLVABLE**
  (nilpotent ideal `⟨D_1,…⟩` extended by the semisimple grading `D_0`), non-nilpotent for
  `n ≥ 3`.
- **`n = char = p`**: the constraint `n·a_0 = 0` is vacuous, `a_0` is free, `dim HH¹ = p`, and
  `HH¹ = Der(k[x]/(x^p)) = W₁`, the **Jacobson–Witt algebra** (basis `D_{-1},…,D_{p-2}`,
  `D_{-1} = ∂`). `W₁` is **simple for `p ≥ 3`** (perfect, Killing degenerate — the char-0
  machinery fails), and the 2-dimensional non-abelian **solvable** algebra for `p = 2`.
- **CARD REFINEMENT (binding, evidence in Live-verified).** R11 writes "W₁ when char|n". The
  sharp statement — live-verified — is at **`n = char = p`**. For `char | n` with `n ≠ p` the
  algebra is a *larger* generalized-Witt/Zassenhaus derivation algebra whose type varies:
  `k[x]/(x⁴)` over `GF(2)` (dim 4) and `k[x]/(x⁶)` over `GF(2)` (dim 6) are **solvable**,
  while `k[x]/(x⁶)` over `GF(3)` (dim 6) is **not** solvable. The plan pins the clean
  `n = p` Witt case and documents the `n ≠ p` subtlety honestly rather than repeating the loose
  "char | n".

### 5. The RSS Ext-quiver solvability criterion (oracle 3; arbitrary characteristic)

For `A = kQ/I` with Gabriel quiver `Q`, `dim Ext¹(S_i, S_j) = #{arrows i → j}` and
`dim Ext¹(S_i, S_i) = #{loops at i}`. RSS (1903.12145, abstract verified): **if `Q` has no
loops and no parallel arrows** (`Ext¹(S,S)=0 ∀S` and `dim Ext¹(S,T) ≤ 1 ∀S,T`) **then `HH¹` is
solvable**, in any characteristic. This is a **pure quiver predicate**, decidable from `Q`
alone — the plan exposes it as `rss_solvable_certificate(A)` returning the verdict + the
witnessing quiver condition, and cross-checks it against the computed `is_solvable_hh1(A)`.
The **negative control** is the Kronecker quiver (parallel arrows) — the criterion does not
apply and `HH¹ = sl₂` is not solvable.

### 6. The card's "T(Kronecker) ≅ sl₂" — refined (binding; evidence below)

- **`kK₂` = the Kronecker path algebra** (`Q = 1 ⇉ 2`, `I = 0`, `dim 4`): **`HH¹(kK₂) ≅ sl₂`
  exactly** — dim 3, perfect, Killing nondegenerate, an explicit **split** sl₂-triple
  `(h,e,f)` with `[h,e]=2e, [h,f]=−2f, [e,f]=h`. Over `GF(2)` it degenerates (Killing rank 0,
  not perfect) — HH¹ ≅ pgl₂, and `pgl₂ ≅ sl₂` **iff char ≠ 2**, reproducing the card's caveat
  precisely. This is the clean sl₂ oracle and matches Eisele–Raedschelders (the sl₂ comes from
  the Kronecker subquiver).
- **`T(kK₂)` = the trivial extension** (`dim 8`): `HH¹(T(kK₂))` has **dim 4 = k ⋉ sl₂** — a
  1-dimensional solvable radical and Levi factor `sl₂` (`sl₂`-count 1). NOT `sl₂` itself. This
  is the CSS 1811.02211 "one low-dimensional non-solvable case" (a Brauer graph algebra =
  trivial extension of a gentle algebra) and fits the Eisele–Raedschelders "solvable ⊕ ⊕sl₂"
  decomposition with one copy.
- **Ruling:** the plan pins **both** — `kK₂` as the exact-`sl₂` literature pin, `T(kK₂)` as the
  trivial-extension/Brauer sl₂-count-1 example — and records the card's "T(Kronecker) ≅ sl₂"
  as **the Levi factor (sl₂-count) of `T(kK₂)`, exactly `sl₂` only for `kK₂` itself**.

## Open design risks (what a critic will likely attack)

1. **The general simple-ideal decomposition / `levi_type` / toral rank.** The hardest,
   least-verified piece; I verified `sl₂`/`k⋉sl₂`/solvable cases directly but not a many-sl₂ or
   mixed-type `S`. *Mitigation:* `sl2_count` falls back to `dim S/3` only after certifying `S`
   is a sum of sl₂'s; otherwise `status` records the incomplete decomposition — never a guessed
   type. The E-R Kronecker-subquiver formula is the cross-check on tame/finite type.
2. **The bracket = commutator identification.** I verified the vector space, not the bracket
   coefficients, against the bar engine. *Mitigation:* Gerstenhaber 1963 (degree-1 = commutator)
   + the shipped Plan-35/P51 `gerstenhaber_brackets` cross-engine invariant check (Task 4).
3. **The char-0 gate rationale.** A critic may argue `char > dim` suffices (as in
   `decompose.py`). It does NOT for Lie theory — there is **no positive-characteristic
   Cartan/Levi/Weyl theorem at all**, so `char > dim` gives no justification regardless of `dim`.
   (Do NOT frame `W₁` as the `char > dim` counterexample: `W₁ = HH¹(k[x]/(x^p))` has
   `char = dim = p`, so it is not a `char > dim` instance — the earlier draft's contrast was
   logically unsound and is removed.) *Mitigation:* documented at the gate + the char-p
   loud-refusal self-cert.
4. **Cost.** The Der solve is `d²×d²`; big algebras are heavy. *Mitigation:* `DEFAULT_MAXDIM`
   budget + honest `status="budget"`; the estimator sizes it above a scalar HH degree.
5. **Base-change honesty — the DEFAULT field is the trap.** The sl₂-count could silently
   undercount an anisotropic form; and the DEFAULT field `CC` is *formally*
   `is_algebraically_closed == True` while its arithmetic is exact QQ, so gating the note on the
   formal flag would SUPPRESS it exactly where it is needed (the MAJOR finding this fix round).
   *Mitigation:* the `base_change_note` is gated on the **arithmetic field** and attached over
   every char-0 quiverlab domain (CC/QQ/`GF(p)`/`GF(pⁿ)`), the CC case worded "formally closed,
   exact QQ arithmetic" — the P62 wording precedent; pinned by `test_default_field_CC_equals_QQ_run`
   and the block-layer default-CC test.
6. **GAP function names.** `IsLieSolvable` vs `IsSolvableLieAlgebra`, `SemiSimpleType` over QQ.
   *Mitigation:* `# PIN`'d to confirm against the installed GAP; the qpa test is an honest skip
   that FAILS if the Lie library disappears.
